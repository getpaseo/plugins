AnuPpuccin adds two color themes to Paseo, AnuPpuccin (dark) and AnuPpuccin Light, using a Catppuccin-style palette, with a lavender accent on dark blue-gray and a blue-violet accent on pale gray. Select one under Settings, Appearance, Theme.
