Baseline adds two themes, Baseline (dark) and Baseline Light. The dark one has a deep navy background with a dark teal accent, and the light one has a warm off-white background with a softer teal accent. Select it in Settings, Appearance, Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
