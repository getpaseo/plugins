Blue Topaz adds two themes, Blue Topaz (dark) and Blue Topaz Light. The dark one is neutral gray with an olive-green accent, and the light one is white with a bright blue accent. Select it in Settings, Appearance, Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
