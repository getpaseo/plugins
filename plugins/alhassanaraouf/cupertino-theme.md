Cupertino adds two themes, Cupertino (dark) and Cupertino Light, both with a bright blue accent. The dark one has a near-black background with white text, and the light one has a white background with black text. Select it in Settings, Appearance, Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
