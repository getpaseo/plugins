Cyber Glow adds two themes, Cyber Glow (dark) and Cyber Glow Light, both with a cyan-blue accent. The dark one has a pure black background, and the light one has a white background with gray raised surfaces. Select it in Settings, Appearance, Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
