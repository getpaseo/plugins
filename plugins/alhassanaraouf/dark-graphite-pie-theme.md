Dark Graphite Pie adds two themes, Dark Graphite Pie (dark) and Dark Graphite Pie Light. The dark one has a graphite background with a blue accent. The light one has a white background with red text and accent, and its raised surfaces are dark. Select it in Settings, Appearance, Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
