Moonlight adds two color themes to Paseo: Moonlight (dark) and Moonlight Light (light). The dark theme uses a dark charcoal background (#202020) with light gray text and a blue-violet accent (#5c73f2); the light theme uses a white background with dark slate text and the same accent. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
