Noctis adds a color theme to Paseo: Noctis, a dark theme. It uses a deep teal background (#052529) with pale cyan-gray text (#b2cacd) and a teal accent (#009999). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
