Notation adds two color themes to Paseo: Notation (dark) and Notation Light (light). The dark theme uses a slate-gray background (#2f3437) with white text and a light blue accent (#2eaadc); the light theme uses a white background with warm dark text (#37352f) and the same accent. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
