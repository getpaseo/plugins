Obsdn-Dark-Rmx adds two color themes to Paseo: Obsdn-Dark-Rmx (dark) and Obsdn-Dark-Rmx Light (light). The dark theme uses a near-black gray background (#212121) with light gray text and a cyan-blue accent (#20abe9); the light theme uses a white background with dark slate text and a similar blue accent (#22b6e2). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
