Obsidian Boom adds a color theme to Paseo: Obsidian Boom, a light theme. It uses a white background with dark blue-gray text (#202b33) and a blue accent (#106ba3). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
