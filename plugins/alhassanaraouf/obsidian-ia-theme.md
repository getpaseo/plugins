obsidian_ia adds two color themes to Paseo: obsidian_ia (dark) and obsidian_ia Light (light). The dark theme uses a near-black green-gray background (#191a19) with light gray text and a bright cyan accent (#00c1ff); the light theme uses an off-white background (#f7f7f7) with near-black text and a cyan accent (#00b1ef). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
