Obsidian Nord adds two color themes to Paseo: Obsidian Nord (dark) and Obsidian Nord Light (light). The dark theme uses a blue-gray background (#2e3440) with off-white text (#eceff4), a muted orange accent (#d08770), and a steel-blue focus ring; the light theme uses a white background with slate text (#434c5e) and the same accent and ring. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
