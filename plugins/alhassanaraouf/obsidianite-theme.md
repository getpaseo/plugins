Obsidianite adds a color theme to Paseo: Obsidianite, a dark theme. It uses a very dark purple background (#100e17) with gray text (#bebebe) and a bright cyan accent (#0ed2f7), which it also uses for borders and the focus ring. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
