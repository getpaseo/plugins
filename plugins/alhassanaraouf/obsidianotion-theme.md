Obsidianotion adds two color themes to Paseo: Obsidianotion (dark) and Obsidianotion Light (light). The dark theme uses a dark gray background (#1f1f1f) with off-white text and a light blue accent (#4dabf7); the light theme uses a white background with warm dark text (#31302e) and a stronger blue accent (#0075de). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
