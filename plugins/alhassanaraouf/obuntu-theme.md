Obuntu adds two color themes to Paseo: Obuntu (dark) and Obuntu Light (light). The dark theme uses a mid-dark gray background (#343434) with light gray text and an orange-red accent (#df4a16); the light theme uses a white background with near-black text and the same accent. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
