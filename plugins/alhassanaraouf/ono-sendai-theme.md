Ono Sendai adds two color themes to Paseo: Ono Sendai (dark) and Ono Sendai Light (light). The dark theme uses a near-black background (#17191a) with a deep blue raised surface (#0f5172) and a cyan accent (#20abe9); the light theme uses a tan background (#d1cabf) with an amber raised surface (#eeb157), a red-orange accent (#e24822), and a steel-blue border. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
