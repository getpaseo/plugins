Origami adds two color themes to Paseo: Origami (dark) and Origami Light (light). The dark theme uses a dark gray background (#202020) with light gray text and a purple accent (#8a5cf5); the light theme uses a warm off-white background (#f8f5f3) with near-black text and the same accent. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
