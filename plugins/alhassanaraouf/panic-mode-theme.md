Panic Mode adds a color theme to Paseo: Panic Mode, a dark theme. It uses a navy background (#151e2d) with pale cream text (#e0e2d5), and gray (#a1a1a1) borders, accent, and muted text. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
