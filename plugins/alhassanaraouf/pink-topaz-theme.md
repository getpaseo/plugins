Pink Topaz adds two color themes to Paseo: Pink Topaz (dark) and Pink Topaz Light (light). The dark theme uses a dark gray background (#212121) with cream text (#efe9d9) and a pink accent (#f39ba0); the light theme uses a cream background (#efe9d9) with dark brown text (#37291a) and a light pink accent (#f5a7a2). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
