Primary adds two color themes to Paseo: Primary (dark) and Primary Light (light). The dark theme uses a dark brown background (#2e261f) with tan text (#d7c0a3) and brown accents; the light theme uses an off-white background (#f8f5f1) with brown text (#593e22) and a taupe accent (#9d8062). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
