Prism adds two color themes to Paseo: Prism (dark) and Prism Light (light). The dark theme uses a deep red background (#481b19) with pale pink text (#f6e5e5) and a red accent (#af4841); the light theme uses a pale pink-white background (#fdf7f7) with dark maroon text (#3b1311) and a salmon accent (#f3a59b). It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later and needs no setup beyond choosing the theme under Settings, Appearance. It has no settings of its own.
