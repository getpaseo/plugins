Retroma and Retroma Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Retroma has a near-black background (#1a1a1e), near-white text (#fafafa), and a green accent (#2d8b62). Retroma Light has a white background, near-black text (#1a1a1e), and the same green accent (#2d8b62). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
