Reverie and Reverie Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Reverie has a dark blue-grey background (#1a2023), cream text (#faf2d6), and a teal accent (#0b797d). Reverie Light has a light grey background (#e7e7e7), dark blue-grey text (#1a2023), and a dark green accent (#0c5249). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
