Tokyo Night and Tokyo Night Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Tokyo Night has a dark blue-black background (#1a1b26), pale blue text (#c0caf5), and a light blue accent (#80d0ff). Tokyo Night Light has a grey background (#d5d6db), navy text (#343b58), and a blue accent (#0f87cc). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
