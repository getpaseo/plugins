Typewriter and Typewriter Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Typewriter has a dark grey background (#262626), tan text (#c5b8a1), and a green accent (#6db478). Typewriter Light has a cream background (#fcf5e4), dark grey text (#262626), and the same green accent (#6db478). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
