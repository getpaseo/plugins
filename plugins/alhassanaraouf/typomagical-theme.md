Typomagical and Typomagical Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Typomagical has a black background, pale blue text (#cdd6f4), and a light blue accent (#89b4fa). Typomagical Light has a light grey background (#eff1f5), slate text (#4c4f69), and an amber accent (#df8e1d). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
