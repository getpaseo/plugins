Typora-Vue and Typora-Vue Light add dark and light themes to Paseo. Choose either one under Settings, Appearance, Theme. Typora-Vue has a dark grey background (#202020), near-white text (#eeeeee), and a green accent (#42b883). Typora-Vue Light has a white background, slate-blue text (#34495e), and the same green accent (#42b883). Requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
