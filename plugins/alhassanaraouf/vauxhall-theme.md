Vauxhall is a color theme for Paseo. The dark variant has a near-black violet background and pale violet text, and the light variant has a pale background and deep violet text. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or newer. Select **Vauxhall** or **Vauxhall Light** under Settings, Appearance, Theme.
