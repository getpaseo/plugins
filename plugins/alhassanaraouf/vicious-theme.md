Vicious is a color theme for Paseo. The dark variant has a near-black blue background and the light variant has an off-white background, both with a pale gold accent. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or newer. Select **Vicious** or **Vicious Light** under Settings, Appearance, Theme.
