Wasp is a color theme for Paseo. The dark variant has a charcoal background and cream text, and the light variant has a warm off-white background and dark brown text, with yellow and amber accents. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or newer. Select **Wasp** or **Wasp Light** under Settings, Appearance, Theme.
