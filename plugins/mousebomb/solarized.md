Solarized adds Solarized Light and Solarized Dark to Paseo, using the Solarized palette: a cream background with orange accents for light, and a deep teal background with cyan accents for dark. Choose either theme under Settings → Appearance → Theme. It requires Paseo 0.8.0 or later.
