Adds four Catppuccin color schemes to Paseo: Latte (light), and Frappé, Macchiato and Mocha (dark). Pick one in Settings → Appearance. Each scheme sets the background, text, raised surfaces, controls, borders, accent, muted text and focus ring colors. The plugin has no settings, needs no setup, and requires Paseo 0.9.0 or newer. It only registers fixed color values and does not read your files, send data or ask for permissions.
