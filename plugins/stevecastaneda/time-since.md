Time Since adds a clock above the composer showing how long ago the agent last replied. It needs Paseo 0.9 or later.

The plugin's daemon side records when each agent's turn ends, so the clock keeps counting from the last reply after you close and reopen Paseo. For turns that ended before the plugin was installed it estimates from older messages, so those readings are older than the real reply.

You can hide the clock icon or add an "ago" suffix from "Time Since Options" in the Command Center. These options apply to all workspaces on the connected host. The options and reply times are stored on the daemon host, under the Paseo home.
